2. Every post covers one actual current effect with exact current controls, an answer box, a tutorial, a parameter table, real-world uses, a download, visible FAQs, structured data, canonical metadata, RSS/sitemap entries, internal links, and original accessible art.
3. Constantly improve SEO and AEO without keyword stuffing. Discoverability is the purpose, but usefulness earns it.
4. Include one currently live aestheticrebellion.store listing as deliberately obvious, funny, sarcastic product placement. Verify the page before publishing.
5. Preserve the crude adult editorial voice without slurs, harassment, unsafe claims, invented sources, or promises that content will go viral.
6. Test source accuracy, production behavior, desktop, mobile, direct URLs, downloads, and build output before publishing.
7. Preserve unrelated work and never overwrite another agent's or Dyles's uncommitted changes.
