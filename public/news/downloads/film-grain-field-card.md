# Ether-MOSH Film Grain field card

## Direct answer

Film Grain adds animated, colored, midtone-weighted texture and a separate warm halo around bright regions. It evokes photographic grain and halation, but it does not identify or simulate a specific stock, camera, scan, emulsion, or preservation condition.

## Starter recipe

- Category: Atmosphere
- Amount: 0.28
- Halation: 0.22
- Source: a portrait, night exterior, archival still, product image, or clip with open midtones and a few bright edges
- Optional stack: light Bloom for projection; Vignette for a restrained finish

## Control map

| Control | Range | What it changes | Practical start |
| --- | ---: | --- | ---: |
| Amount | 0–1 | Animated multiscale and colored texture, strongest through the midtones | 0.28 |
| Halation | 0–1 | Warm orange-red bleed around bright source regions | 0.22 |

## Production notes

1. Judge animated grain while the source plays and at final viewing size.
2. Keep essential text, controls, safety information, and logos outside the effected layer.
3. Inspect still exports at 100% and proof on the intended paper or textile.
4. Check a 3×3 repeat before manufacturing a pattern.
5. Test projectors and LED walls on the real surface; fine texture may disappear or alias.
6. Avoid rapid high-contrast cuts, and provide a clean or lower-intensity alternate when needed.
7. Do not present a creative Film Grain pass as film-stock identification, archival evidence, or restoration.
