# Ether-MOSH Field Card: Forge Mode

## Fast recipe

- Source: Flame icon → Forge
- Generators: Volumetric Bloom, Drift Field, Shatter Field, Pour Bloom, Contour Bands, Reaction Diffusion
- Intensity: 0.42–0.72
- Best practice: keep the seed after a strong draw
- Useful options: audio response, base image, mosaic overlay, seamless output

## Production checklist

- Choose the generator family before heavily editing the effect stack.
- Let the crossfade finish before judging a new state.
- Use audio response for performance and a stable state for print.
- Enable Seamless before composing a repeat.
- Export a loop, still, tile, or 2048–8000 px print-ready PNG at the selected DPI.
