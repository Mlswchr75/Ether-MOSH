# Ether-MOSH Oil Slick field card

## Direct answer

Oil Slick maps source luminance through three animated cosine color bands and blends that result over the image. It evokes thin-film iridescence, but it does not simulate film thickness, refractive index, wavelength, reflection phase, or viewing angle.

## Starter recipe

- Category: Color Chaos
- Amount: 0.45
- Frequency: 6
- Source: a portrait, sculpture, glass, fabric, or architecture with clear highlights and shadows
- Optional stack: light Bloom for projection; Film Grain for stills

## Control map

| Control | Range | What it changes | Practical start |
| --- | ---: | --- | ---: |
| Amount | 0–1 | Visible intensity; 0 is neutral, 0.50 reaches full shader blend, and higher values amplify the treatment | 0.45 |
| Frequency | 1–20 | Number of color cycles across source luminance | 6 |

## Production notes

1. Use Frequency 3–6 for broad fields and 8–12 for dense contours.
2. Keep meaningful text, controls, safety information, and logos outside the effected layer.
3. Inspect saturated gradients at 100% and soft-proof print output.
4. Check a 3×3 pattern proof before manufacturing.
5. For projection, avoid rapid Amount cuts, test the real display for flash risk, and provide a static or lower-intensity alternate.
6. A real rainbow sheen on outdoor water may indicate pollution. Follow local reporting guidance; do not create a spill for reference material.
