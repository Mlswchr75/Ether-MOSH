# Ether-MOSH Duotone field card

## Quick recipe

- Category: Color
- Mix: 0.80
- Hue: 0.85
- Source: strong grayscale silhouette with distinct shadows and highlights
- Optional stack: Posterize before Duotone; low Bloom after it

## Control map

| Control | Range | What it changes |
| --- | ---: | --- |
| Mix | 0–1 | Blends the source with the two-color luminance map |
| Hue | 0–1 | Chooses the dark hue; the bright hue sits opposite |

## Production checklist

1. Start at 0.80 Mix and 0.85 Hue.
2. Move Hue slowly and check that both shadows and highlights remain distinct.
3. Lower Mix for a tint or raise it for a dominant mapped palette.
4. Keep labels, icons, and state cues outside the effected layer.
5. Proof the exported palette on the destination screen or stock.
6. Rebuild real spot-ink separations and curves with the printer.

## Accessibility note

Do not use the two colors as the only way to communicate meaning. Preserve text, shape, pattern, or another non-color cue and verify contrast separately.
