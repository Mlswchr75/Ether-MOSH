# Ether-MOSH Kaleidoscope field card

## Quick recipe

- Category: Geometry
- Segments: 6
- Spin: 0.15
- Source: one clear off-center subject or asymmetrical edge
- Optional stack: Duotone before Kaleidoscope; Bloom after it at a low amount

## Control map

| Control | Range | What it changes |
| --- | ---: | --- |
| Segments | 2–16 | Number and density of mirrored angular wedges |
| Spin | 0–1 | Continuous rotation speed; 0 holds the fold still |

## Production checklist

1. Frame the source asymmetrically so the repeated wedges have something useful to multiply.
2. Start at 6 Segments and 0.15 Spin; judge the output at its final size.
3. Use Spin 0 for print, pattern input, held installation states, or a lower-motion version.
4. For projection, rehearse at full brightness and avoid rapid flashes or hard luminance cuts elsewhere in the stack.
5. For fabric or wallpaper, use the still as a Motif source and inspect the 3×3 repeat proof.
6. For vector or Lottie work, trace only the dominant wedge boundaries or mask a short low-Spin raster loop.

## Accessibility note

Kaleidoscope does not generate flashes by itself, but fast rotation and high-contrast or flashing layers can cause discomfort. Keep public-facing motion slow, provide a static option when practical, and evaluate flashing output against WCAG 2.2 Success Criterion 2.3.1.
