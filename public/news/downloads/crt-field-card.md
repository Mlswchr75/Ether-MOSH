# Ether-MOSH Field Card: CRT

## Fast recipe

- Category: Atmosphere
- Curvature: 0.20–0.48
- Shadow Mask: 0.35–0.65
- Bloom: 0.18–0.45
- Best sources: bright edges, type, faces, games, dark backgrounds

## Production checklist

- Set Curvature first, Shadow Mask second, and Bloom last.
- Judge the phosphor structure at the final display or output size.
- Reduce Shadow Mask if scaling or compression makes it shimmer.
- Test on the real projector or LED processor before show time.
- For print, proof fine stripes for unintended moire.
